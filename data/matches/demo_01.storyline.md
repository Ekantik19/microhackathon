# demo_01 storyline: Northbridge FC 1–1 Riverside Athletic

Synthetic match data. Clubs and players are fictional. Generated by
`python -m app.events.generator --seed 42 --match-id demo_01` (run from `backend/`).

## Clock

- `match_second` is continuous. 1st half: 0–2820 s (45' + 2:00 stoppage). 2nd half: 2820–5760 s (45' + 4:00 stoppage).
- The display clock restarts at 45:00 for the 2nd half, so 69' = `match_second` 2820 + 24 × 60 = 4260.
- Coordinates are normalized so the acting team always attacks toward x = 105.

## Scripted beats

These are written as code in `MatchBuilder.beats()` (`backend/app/events/generator.py`) and happen at exactly these
times for every seed. Background play between beats is random but seeded.

the scripted ones.

## Synthetic xG

`backend/app/events/xg.py`: a logistic function of distance to goal and the angle subtended by the posts. It is
a documented formula, not a trained or validated model, and is labeled "synthetic xG" everywhere.
